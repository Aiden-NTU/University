# Team and contributions

## Roles

The brief asks each team to share five roles [@ntu_brief]. The Project Manager keeps the project
on schedule and is the main contact for the lab leader. The Design Lead is responsible for the
spatial design and drawings. The Sustainability & Research Lead investigates materials and
technologies and leads this Design Outline. The Visual Communications Lead leads the poster.
The Cost & Resource Coordinator estimates costs and researches suppliers.
