# Executive summary

This Design Outline presents **The Heat Exchange**, a student educational resource centre built
to replace the existing Clifton Campus car park. The design responds to the Unit 1 Sustainable
Design brief by integrating efficient heat-management solutions, renewable energy sources and a
renewed natural landscape [@ntu_brief]. Through those choices we address clusters of the United
Nations Sustainable Development Goals: Goals 3–5 (health, education and equality), Goals 6–9
(water, energy, work and infrastructure) and Goals 13–17 (climate action and partnership)
[@un2015]. How individual features map to specific targets is set out in
[[Materials, research and the SDGs]].

We express the project in several accessible formats, so different audiences can engage with it:

- A **physical model** at 1:1000 of the surrounding terrain, beside a 1:100 “doll’s house” building
  whose floors lift off so visitors can see rooms and furniture on each level.
- An **interactive web twin**, hosted on GitHub Pages and linked from a QR code on the poster,
  so people can walk the landscape and building in their own time.
- This **Design Outline**, plus four **laser-engraved plywood walls** that slot around the site
  model and carry the quick-read project facts.
- A **Google Earth** placement of the building in its exact campus position.

Those routes are deliberate. They show that a design can be communicated interactively, across
media, rather than only as a paper poster.
